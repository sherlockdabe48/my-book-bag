MyBookBag

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Overview

MyBookBag is a personal reading tracker. We take your privacy seriously — the app is designed to work entirely on your device without collecting, transmitting, or sharing any personal information.

Data we do not collect

MyBookBag does not collect or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data that could personally identify you

Data stored on your device

All your reading data — your shelf, bag, book notes, reading progress, tags, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, using your browser's local storage or the app's on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you use the Export feature to manually save a backup file to your own device.

Book search

When you search for books, your search query is sent to the Open Library API (openlibrary.org), a free public service operated by the Internet Archive. These requests contain only the search text you type — no personal information is included. Please refer to the Internet Archive's terms for their data practices.

Third-party services

MyBookBag does not integrate any of the following:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics platforms (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Social login or third-party authentication
  • Payment processors

Children's privacy

MyBookBag does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no account registration and stores no data outside of the user's own device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
